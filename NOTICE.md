## Accuracy disclaimer

The reference data bundled with this app (races, classes, feats, spells, equipment,
companion base statistics, ability/save/BAB progressions, etc.) was reconstructed from
general knowledge of the ruleset for the purpose of a helper tool, not transcribed
field-by-field from the rulebook. Numeric tables that are easy to get subtly wrong —
weapon/armor costs, exact companion base stat blocks, and anything level-by-level beyond
the well-known BAB/save formulas — should be spot-checked against the Core Rulebook or the
official Pathfinder SRD before you rely on them for organized/tournament play.
